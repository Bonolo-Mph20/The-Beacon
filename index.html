About Lesedi

Poise on stage, presence off it.

Lesedi Monareng is a model and pageant titleholder based in the Free State, South Africa. What started as a first walk across a local stage has grown into a fast-rising modelling career — one built on discipline, warmth, and an unmistakable presence in front of the camera.

Represented by SB Modelling Agency and Vision Shelomi Modelling Agency, Lesedi has carried titles including MCF 1st Princess and Miss Summer Sensational, and continues to compete and shoot across the region. She's drawn to work that lets her tell a story — whether that's a tiara and sash on stage, or something quieter in front of the lens.

— Lesedi
